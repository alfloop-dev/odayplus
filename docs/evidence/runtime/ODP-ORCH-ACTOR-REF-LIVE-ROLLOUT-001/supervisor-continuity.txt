## why the atomic path matters here

    open fds under /proc/1487837/fd naming ai_status.py: 0
  The real readers are short-lived — every worker CLI invocation and every
  Supervisor-spawned status command opens the file fresh, at arbitrary moments.
  'install -m' truncates and rewrites the target in place, so a reader that
  opens mid-write sees a partial file. 'os.replace' publishes by rename(2):
  each open resolves to the whole old inode or the whole new one, never a
  half-written file. That is the property this round adds; the bytes were
  already correct after round 1.
